Community Standards

Community Code of Conduct

Promoting an Inclusive, Safe, and Collaborative Learning Environment

1. Our Pledge

HackHub is dedicated to providing a respectful, harassment-free hackathon experience for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, or tech stack preference.

2. Expected Behavior

  • Be respectful and collaborative with teammates, competitors, mentors, and judges.
  • Provide constructive, encouraging feedback during peer reviews and live project pitches.
  • Respect venue rules, mentor office hours, and equipment during in-person college events.
  • Uphold academic honesty by giving proper attribution to open-source libraries and APIs used.

3. Unacceptable Behavior

  • Harassment, offensive remarks, or discriminatory language in team chats, project titles, or announcements.
  • Plagiarism, submitting pre-existing commercial projects, or claiming others' work as original.
  • Tampering with scoring systems, automated bot voting, or harassment of evaluation panels.

4. Reporting & Enforcement

Participants violating these standards will be warned, removed from teams, or permanently blocked by event administrators. Reports can be filed directly with event organisers or through platform administrators.
