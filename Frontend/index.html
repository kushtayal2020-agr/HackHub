How HackHub Works

A streamlined, end-to-end lifecycle for every hackathon milestone.

1

Discover & Assemble

Browse approved college hackathons. Register as a participant, create your team, and invite members with unique shareable invite codes.

2

Check In & Build

Arrive at the venue and scan your individual check-in QR code. Submit your GitHub repository, live demo URL, and project documentation.

3

Judge & Download

Expert judges score submissions using weighted criteria. Track live scores on the real-time leaderboard and download official PDF certificates.

Enterprise-Grade Hackathon Infrastructure

Purpose-built tools designed to make organizing, participating, and judging effortless.

Team Management

Automated invite codes, capacity enforcement, member controls, and prevention of duplicate registrations.

QR Venue Check-In

Instant QR ticket generation for attendees with organizer-side camera scanner and live attendance tracking.

Live Leaderboard

Real-time WebSocket score sync powered by Socket.io, weighted criterion averages, and tie-breaking algorithms.

Verifiable Certificates

Automated vector PDF certificate generation for podium winners and verified participants upon results release.
