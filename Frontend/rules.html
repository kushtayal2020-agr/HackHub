Hackathon Rules & Guidelines

Standard Event Rules for HackHub Hackathons • Version 2026

1. Team Formation Rules

  • Teams must be formed within the specified team size limit (default: 1 to 4 members).
  • Team leaders generate a unique alphanumeric invite code to share with teammates.
  • A participant may only join one team per hackathon. Team switching is prohibited after registration closes.
  • Once a hackathon begins, team members cannot leave or be removed by the team leader.

2. Project Submissions

  • All project work must be created during the official hackathon duration (between start date and end date).
  • Submissions must include a valid public GitHub repository and optional demonstration URL.
  • Uploaded project files must not exceed 10 MB and must be in allowed formats (ZIP, PDF, PNG, JPG). Executables are automatically rejected.
  • Only one submission is permitted per team; updating a submission replaces the previous version.

3. Venue Check-In (QR Code Verification)

  • Every registered participant receives an individual encrypted QR check-in ticket in their team portal.
  • Attendees must present this QR code to event volunteers/organisers upon arrival at the venue for check-in confirmation.

4. Scoring & Live Leaderboards

  • Assigned judges score each project across standardized weighted criteria totaling 100%.
  • A team's final score is the mathematical average of all assigned judges' weighted scores.
  • Results remain hidden until the event organiser officially publishes the final leaderboard.

5. Certificates & Recognition

  • Positions 1, 2, and 3 receive official "Certificate of Achievement".
  • All other verified participants with valid submissions receive an official "Certificate of Participation".
